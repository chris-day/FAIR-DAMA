# Data Handling Ethics

Worksheet name: **DataHandlingEthics**. This additional DMBOK topic supplements the 11 core knowledge areas. The source identifies this as section 14.

Source: [DAMA activity diagram](../../assets/images/01_DataHandlingEthics.webp); activity analysis in the [updated workbook](../../assets/downloads/DAMA-PDOC_FAIR-MASTER_2026-11-16_V3.xlsx). Goals, activity labels, codes, deliverable labels and role lists are transcribed from the diagram. Definitions are working summaries; per-activity deliverable associations, role assignments and FAIR interpretations are proposed.

## Definition

Working summary: Establish, communicate and monitor acceptable data-handling practices to manage ethical risk and sustain responsible organizational behavior.

## Goals

1. To control risk by establishing acceptable practices for data handling.
2. To change/instill preferred culture and behaviors on handling data.
3. To maintain alignment with compliant practices in ethical handling of data.

## FAIR Alignment

No direct FAIR principle ID is established by the ethical-practice activities in the image. Proposed indirect links concern authentication and authorization ([A1.2][fair-a1.2]) and clear usage licensing ([R1.1][fair-r1.1]), where explicit ethical policies and risk controls commission these requirements. Ethical acceptability, privacy, consent and organizational culture are important considerations beyond the FAIR requirements. An ethics policy or training completion is not evidence of FAIR fulfilment.

## Business Drivers

### Inputs

- Existing and Preferred Organization Ethics
- Business Strategy & Goals
- Organizational Structure
- Business Culture
- Regulations
- Existing Corporate Policies

### Activities

1. Review Data-Handling Practices (P)
2. Identify Principles, Practices, and Risk Factors (P)
3. Create an Ethical Data Handling Strategy (P)
4. Identify and Remediate Gaps in Existing Practices (D)
5. Execute the Training and Communications Plan (D)
6. Implement Risk Mitigation (D)
7. Implement Monitoring Plan (C)

- Communication Plan
