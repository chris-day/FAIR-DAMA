# DAMA-DMBOK Core Knowledge Areas

The following table summarizes the 11 core knowledge areas of DAMA-DMBOK2 and the role each plays in managing data throughout its lifecycle. It provides a common reference for the implementation-oriented FAIR alignment later in this document. These 11 areas reflect the DMBOK2 structure; the earlier source images and worksheet-based mappings retain their original ten-segment structure.

| Area name | High-level description |
| --- | --- |
| **[Data Governance][dama-01]** | Establishes policies, roles, and standards for managing data as a valuable business asset. Ensures compliance, accountability, and consistency across the organization. |
| **[Data Architecture][dama-02]** | Designs and maintains data structures and frameworks that support business strategy. Includes defining data models, selecting technologies, and aligning data flows with enterprise goals. |
| **Data Modeling & Design** | Develops conceptual, logical, and physical data representations to support business operations and analytics, enabling better integration and usability. |
| **[Data Storage & Operations][dama-04]** | Ensures data is stored, retrieved, and maintained efficiently through database management, backup and recovery, and performance optimization. |
| **[Data Security][dama-05]** | Protects data from unauthorized access, breaches, and loss through access controls, encryption, regulatory compliance, and security policies. |
| **[Data Integration & Interoperability][dama-11]** | Enables data flow across systems and platforms through Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) processes, API-based integrations, and consistent data formats. |
| **[Documents & Content Management][dama-08]** | Manages unstructured data, including documents, images, videos, and other content, ensuring digital assets are stored, categorized, and accessed according to business needs. |
| **[Reference & Master Data Management][dama-06]** | Ensures consistency in core entities such as customers, products, and locations across business units. Provides a shared, authoritative source for key business information. |
| **[Data Warehousing & Business Intelligence][dama-07]** | Supports analytics, reporting, and decision-making by organizing data for access through data warehouses, data lakes, and business intelligence tools. |
| **[Metadata Management][dama-09]** | Maintains data descriptions to improve understanding, governance, and accessibility. Uses metadata catalogs to track lineage, definitions, and usage across systems. |
| **[Data Quality Management][dama-10]** | Ensures data is accurate, complete, consistent, timely, valid, and unique through quality metrics, error detection, cleansing, and enrichment. |
